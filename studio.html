About Me

My background is in IT support, and I have a bachelor’s degree in Information Systems. Working with technology from the support side sparked my interest in how applications work and how people depend on them.

Through CourDevelops, I’m developing my own projects—from a timekeeping application to a workspace for creative projects. I’m especially interested in how interfaces, backend logic, and data come together to support everyday tasks.

This portfolio documents that work as it develops. I share what’s working, what needs improvement, and the decisions that shape each project.

What Interests Me

Applications that help people manage everyday work.

How I Work
01

Understand

Understand the problem, the workflow, and the people involved. I identify the key pain points and opportunities for improvement, and define the goals for the system.

02

Build

I create a working prototype to test the workflow and validate the design. I iterate on the prototype until it meets the goals and requirements.

03

Use AI

I use AI to explore approaches, troubleshoot issues, and review changes. I’m responsible for understanding the code, checking its behavior, and deciding what belongs in the project.

04

Test

I test the system with real users and gather feedback. I identify any issues or areas for improvement, and refine the system accordingly.

05

Refine

I continue to refine the system based on feedback and testing. I ensure that the system is reliable, scalable, and easy to use.
