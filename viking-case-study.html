Verified milestones

Account cooldown and recovery

I tested three outcomes on the PostgreSQL backend branch. A test account entered cooldown after repeated incorrect PIN attempts. A second account could still sign in. The original account could sign in again after the cooldown expired.

Developer Thinking

I wanted a cooldown to apply to the account receiving repeated failed attempts. That decision gave me two acceptance checks beyond displaying the message: whether a second employee could still sign in and whether the cooled-down employee could get back in later.
